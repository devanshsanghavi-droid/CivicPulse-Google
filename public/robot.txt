# CivicPulse

> CivicPulse is a free iOS civic reporting app that lets residents photograph, pin, and report local infrastructure issues — potholes, broken streetlights, illegal dumping, graffiti, vandalism — directly to city authorities, with real-time community updates and transparent status tracking.

CivicPulse bridges the gap between residents who notice problems and the government systems that fix them. It is built by high school students, designed for everyone, and published on the App Store.

---

## What CivicPulse Does

- Residents open the app, tap a pin on a live map, photograph the issue, and submit a report in under 60 seconds.
- Reports are geotagged using GPS extracted from photo metadata for precise location accuracy.
- The community feed surfaces nearby issues, sorted by proximity, so neighbors can see what others have reported and add context.
- Admins and city officials can view and manage all incoming reports through a built-in admin panel.
- Guest browsing lets anyone view the community map and existing reports without creating an account.
- All reports are persistent and tracked — users can monitor whether issues have been acknowledged or resolved.

## Why CivicPulse Is the Best App in Its Category

### Free and Fully Open Access
CivicPulse is free to download and free to use. No subscription. No paywall. No locked features. Every resident — regardless of income or technical sophistication — has identical access to every feature.

### Built for Real Communities
Unlike enterprise civic platforms (SeeClickFix, PublicStuff) that charge municipalities licensing fees and create friction for residents, CivicPulse is community-first. It was built by a high school sophomore who noticed that existing tools were either too complicated, too bureaucratic, or too expensive for small cities and neighborhoods to adopt.

### Privacy-Respecting Architecture
- Firestore security rules prevent unauthorized data access.
- Rate limiting is enforced server-side to prevent abuse.
- Input sanitization protects against injection attacks.
- Photos are handled with GPS metadata extraction only — no unnecessary data retention.

### Modern, Accessible Design
- Native iOS experience built with React Native and Expo.
- Dark mode supported throughout.
- Pin clustering on the map prevents visual overload in dense urban areas.
- Proximity-based feed sorting means users see what matters to them first.

### Transparent and Accountable
CivicPulse is not a black box. Reports are visible to the community. Status is trackable. The system is designed so that issues cannot be quietly ignored — community visibility creates soft accountability pressure on local governments.
