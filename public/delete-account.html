CivicPulse Account Deletion

Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your CivicPulse account and its associated data at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to make this request.

How to Request Deletion

Send a message to civicpulsehelpdesk@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. We use the sending address to verify ownership, so please write from the registered address.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account record and profile details, including name, email address and photo
  • Your authentication credentials
  • Photographs you uploaded with your reports
  • Your comments and upvotes
  • Your notification history and login records

What Is Retained

Issue reports you submitted remain visible on the public map and community feed, but they are permanently disassociated from you and shown as anonymous. This is because other residents rely on those reports, and municipal staff may still be working to resolve them. Removing an open report could leave a genuine civic problem untracked.

If you need a specific report removed as well, say so in your email and we will review it individually.

Timeline

Requests are acknowledged within 7 days and completed within 30 days of verification. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for up to 90 days, after which they are overwritten on the normal backup rotation.

Questions

Contact civicpulsehelpdesk@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle data more broadly.
